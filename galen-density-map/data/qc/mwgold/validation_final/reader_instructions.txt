MW-GOLD VALIDATION SHEET (Sanskrit)

Each row shows a Monier-Williams headword (IAST; compounds hyphenated as MW prints them) and a Sanskrit word in brackets inside its sentence. The sentence is given in sandhi-resolved words as the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit segments it: compounds appear as separate members, so a bracketed span of two or more words is one compound. Answer is_instance = yes if the bracketed word (or the compound the bracketed members form) is a form of that headword (any inflection, any gender; a derivative entered separately by MW, such as a past participle or an adverb, counts as its own headword), no if it is a form of some other word, unsure if the context does not let you decide. If no, give the headword the word really belongs to. Use only the sheet and your knowledge of Sanskrit: no lemmatizer, no dictionary file, no web, no other file. Answer every row. Rows 80: random loci plus salted controls at unmarked positions.
