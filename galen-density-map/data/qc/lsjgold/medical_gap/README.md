## The honest frame

- This is the LSJ-cited stratum: loci exist only where LSJ cites Hippocrates or Galen under a headword and the citation could be relocated in the token stream. It says nothing about words LSJ does not cite. Galen contributes only 28 loci here (LSJ cites little of him).
- Dilemma's agreement is a lexicon's, not a human's: `dilemma_blind == 1` means Dilemma's lemma equals the LSJ headword, accent-blind. Both can be wrong about the token. The training candidates are therefore candidates.
- Relocation was validated blind on a random sample of 60 loci (whole benchmark, not this subset) by two readers (`validation_v03/validation_summary.json`): reader 1 57/60 yes (95.0%, Wilson 95% 86.3-98.29), reader 2 56/60 yes (93.33%, 84.07-97.38); both passed their anchor/plant gates; kappa 0.8485. The sample was not stratified by author, so it is not a medical-subset precision.

## For maintainers

`medical_gap_loci.csv` lists every Hippocrates and Galen locus where one of four neural lemmatizers disagrees with LSJ, with each system's lemma. `medical_gap_headwords.csv` aggregates it per headword. `medical_gap_train_candidates.jsonl` is the subset where Dilemma also agrees with LSJ, shaped as surface + gold headword + 8 tokens of context either side + provenance, in case you want training or test material. These files are a report: not a patch, not a gold standard certified by a human, not a claim that LSJ is right where a system differs (a system may be right and the LSJ headword a variant or alternative lemmatisation), and not covering words LSJ does not cite. Use them to test, to find your own errors, or to ignore. Nothing here was sent to anyone.
