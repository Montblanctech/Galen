# LS-Gold v0.1 — pre-registered relocation change: the quote as anchor (not yet run)

**Observation (v0, 2026-11-08).** 70,169 of the 253,107 held citations end `not_located`: the ±1 window around the cited unit holds no token either oracle reads as the headword. The list is headed by common words (*volo* 258, *bonus* 166, *contra* 153, *bene* 149, *tum* 149, *cum* 143, *ago* 137 …), which the oracles certainly analyse, so the token is not in the window: the locator drifted (L&S cites older editions; Plautus and Terence by scene-relative line; Cicero's speeches by older section numbers). 59.7% of these citations carry a `<quote>`, a run of two to eight words of the cited text.

**Rule to test.** For a `not_located` citation that has a quote of ≥ 2 tokens: search the cited unit ± 3 siblings for the quote's token sequence (keys compared with `latnorm.key`, order kept, one token of the quote may be absent); if the sequence occurs exactly once, the candidate tokens are the quote's tokens at that position, validated by the same three routes as v0 (collatinus / whitaker / quoted-form); exactly one validated token → gold locus with `validation` suffixed `_q` (e.g. `collatinus_q`) so the v0 and v0.1 strata stay separable. No change to located v0 loci.

**Predictions, written before the run.** (1) Recovers between 15,000 and 30,000 loci (a fifth to two fifths of the not-located group with a quote). (2) The recovered loci are not easier for the systems: each system's blind match on the `_q` stratum is within 2 points of its figure on the v0 loci of the same author. (3) A fresh blind sheet (60 random `_q` loci + 20 controls, two readers) passes the gate and shows relocation precision ≥ 95%.

**What a failure looks like.** Prediction 1 fails if fewer than 10,000 loci come back (the quotes are then mostly from different editions' text, and the wider window is noise). Prediction 2 fails if the `_q` stratum is > 2 points easier (the quote preference selects for frequent words). Prediction 3 fails if either reader's precision on the random `_q` loci falls below 95%: the rule is then withdrawn and the 70k stay `not_located`.

**Run order.** `relocate_la.py --quote-anchor` (flag to add), `score_la.py`, `validation_sheet_la.py --only-q --out validation_v01`, two readers, `validation_score.py`. Results go under `## v0.1` in the README whatever they are.
