# What the drafts say: findings catalogue (2026-11-11)

678 items found in the machine drafts of the thirteen Galen First Drafts works by six model readers (Opus), one per group of works, each told to note only what a passage actually says, to copy a quotation verbatim from the draft (rendering A), and to copy 1–4 Greek words from the same passage. Categories: people, lost authors and works, Galen's own works and life, recipes, cases, procedures, places and events, daily life (trade, prices, measures), claims and arguments, and doubts (places where the draft or the transmitted Greek may be wrong).

Checked by `pipeline/firstdrafts/findings_check.py`: the passage exists, the quotation is verbatim in that passage's draft, every Greek word is in that passage's Greek. 678 of 678 passed. A random sample of 12 was read against quote and Greek by the build session: the summaries said what the quotes say. Not checked: whether a summary is complete or right about the Greek beyond the quoted words, and the readers' notes (outside knowledge is confined to `note` and flagged there). The drafts themselves are unverified; items from passages whose checks failed carry that verdict.

Files: `findings.jsonl` / `findings.csv` (kept items), `dropped.jsonl` (none), `readers/` (each reader's raw output). Page: `docs/findings/` (`findings_page.py`).
