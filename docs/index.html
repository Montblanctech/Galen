Galen Density Project — pages

Galen in English for the first time

Nine works of Galen that had no complete English translation, published as machine first drafts by William Pepperman. Each page sets the Greek beside two model renderings, ties every technical term to its LSJ entry, and marks the units that failed the machine checks. They are unverified: drafts for scholars to correct, not translations to quote. All nine, with how they were made.

  • Against Lycus Adversus Lycum
  • On the Composition of Drugs by Kind De compositione medicamentorum per genera
  • On Leeches, Revulsion, Cupping, Incision and Scarification De hirudinibus, revulsione, cucurbitula, incisione et scarificatione
  • Whom to Purge, with Which Cathartic Drugs, and When Quos quibus catharticis medicamentis et quando purgare oporteat
  • On the Power of Purgative Drugs De purgantium medicamentorum facultate
  • On the Periods of the Whole Disease De totius morbi temporibus
  • Commentary on Hippocrates' (or Polybus') Regimen in Health for Private Persons In Hippocratis vel Polybi opus De salubri victus ratione privatorum commentarius
  • On the Shoulder Dislocated in Ways Hippocrates Did Not See De humero iis modis prolapso quos Hippocrates non vidit
  • On Difficulty of Breathing De difficultate respirationis

Tools and benchmarks

  • LSJ-Gold leaderboard — lemmatizer accuracy on the dictionary-cited stratum of Ancient Greek (93,926 relocated loci over 54 authors, CC BY 4.0). JSON.
  • LS-Gold leaderboard — the Latin twin: lemmatizer accuracy on the words Lewis & Short cites (161,032 relocated loci over 33 authors, validated by Collatinus and Whitaker's Words, CC BY-SA 4.0). JSON.
  • Galen Term Explorer — any Galenic word: keyness, LSJ tier, Dilemma headword, punch-list label, contexts, Kühn loci.
  • Term Explorer for further authors — Diophantus, John Philoponus, Philodemus.
