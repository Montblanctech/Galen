Galen Density Project

Galen First Drafts

Nine works of Galen with no complete English translation that we know of, five of them with no complete translation in any modern language (each work's page names the earlier ones), put into English by two language models and checked by machine. Drafts for reading and for scholars to correct, not translations to quote.
