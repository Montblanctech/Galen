On the Composition of Drugs by Kind

De compositione medicamentorum per genera · Kühn XIII 362-1058

The first complete English translation we know of. No complete translation in any modern language. Parts exist in English: G. Baker 1574 (book 3, from a French epitome), M. Marai 2023 (substantial excerpts, Warwick PhD thesis) and ten reference passages (galen_project, 2026). This is rendering A, made by a language model and not yet checked by a scholar. The small grey numbers are the pages of Kühn's Greek edition. Passages with a red rule failed the machine checks; those checks are automated agreement tests, not a reading of the Greek, so a passage without the rule is not checked either. Words in square brackets were supplied by the model for the sense; Greek in parentheses gives the original word; any remark about a doubtful or corrupt reading is the model's, not a scholar's.
