LSJ-Gold leaderboard

Lemmatizer accuracy on the dictionary-cited stratum of Ancient Greek: tokens that Liddell–Scott–Jones cites for a headword, relocated into First1KGreek editions. Version 1.0 (70033 loci, 2026-11-11). Numbers never change inside a version; a new author or system is a new version.

Stable release (1.0, 2026-11): every locus with a reason for doubt was read blind by two readers with salted controls, a third deciding splits: the loci every system misses, the loci only Dilemma gets right, the tokens gold under two headwords, and all that 0.1.2 had dropped or set aside (3,249 loci in two rounds, every control gate passed). 70,033 loci remain; the strata not read one by one were sampled (900 loci) and their residual error is stated in the release card. 1.0 is frozen: any later correction is a numbered erratum, decided the same way. Every system is rescored on the same outputs.

How the loci were made

window = cited unit ±1 sibling; validation morpheus (headword in analyses, accent-blind) else quoted-form equality (sharing the headword's first two letters, accent-blind) when Morpheus has no analysis; exactly one validated surface -> gold. Of 249090 LSJ citations into works held here: located 93926 (LSJ-Gold 1.0 keeps 70,033 of them: from 0.1.1 on, a quoted locus counts only when LSJ quotes that single word, not when the token matches one word of a quoted phrase; from 0.1.2 on, a locus repeated in LSJ counts once; in 1.0 every doubtful locus was read blind and decided by two or three readers), ambiguous 11943, not located 117045 (mostly Morpheus coverage, being filled), locator not in our edition 26176. Match = the system's lemma equals the LSJ headword after accent/case normalisation; strict = exact; article-neutral maps the article's forms to ὁ before comparing.

Honesty

LSJ cites what a 1940 lexicographer found worth citing: this is the rare, dictionary-worthy stratum, not overall accuracy. The Perseus LSJ XML is incomplete. Only works held in First1KGreek or Perseus editions are covered. odyCy's lemmas are a frozen 2026-07 run on First1K and a token-by-token run on the Perseus works; Dilemma is context-free on the surface; convention differences count against a system in the strict and blind columns and are partly undone in the article-neutral one. Relocation checked blind on the v0 loci (6,116) by two model readers on a salted sheet: fable-5-1 60/60 (100.0%, controls 10/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); opus-5-5 60/60 (100.0%, controls 10/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); agreement κ undefined (no variation: both readers said yes to every random locus). Relocation checked blind on the v0.3 loci (96,462) by two model readers on a salted sheet: opus-5-5 57/60 (95.0%, controls 9/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); sonnet-5-5 56/60 (93.33%, controls 10/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); agreement κ 0.8485. No human reader has judged a sheet yet; the sheets and keys are in the repository.
