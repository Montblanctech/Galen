How the loci were made

window = cited unit ±1 sibling; validation morpheus (headword in analyses, accent-blind) else quoted-form equality when Morpheus has no analysis; exactly one validated surface -> gold. Of 168946 LSJ citations into works held here: located 57278, ambiguous 7632, not located 85841 (mostly Morpheus coverage, being filled), locator not in our edition 18195. Match = the system's lemma equals the LSJ headword after accent/case normalisation; strict = exact; article-neutral maps the article's forms to ὁ before comparing.

Honesty

LSJ cites what a 1940 lexicographer found worth citing: this is the rare, dictionary-worthy stratum, not overall accuracy. The Perseus LSJ XML is incomplete. Only works held in First1K editions are covered. odyCy's lemmas are a frozen 2026-07 run; Dilemma is context-free on the surface; convention differences count against a system in the strict and blind columns and are partly undone in the article-neutral one. No human has validated the relocation yet; the blind salted sheet is in the repository.
