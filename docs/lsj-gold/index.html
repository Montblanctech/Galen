How the loci were made

window = cited unit ±1 sibling; validation morpheus (headword in analyses, accent-blind) else quoted-form equality (sharing the headword's first two letters, accent-blind) when Morpheus has no analysis; exactly one validated surface -> gold. Of 249090 LSJ citations into works held here: located 93926, ambiguous 11943, not located 117045 (mostly Morpheus coverage, being filled), locator not in our edition 26176. Match = the system's lemma equals the LSJ headword after accent/case normalisation; strict = exact; article-neutral maps the article's forms to ὁ before comparing.

Honesty

LSJ cites what a 1940 lexicographer found worth citing: this is the rare, dictionary-worthy stratum, not overall accuracy. The Perseus LSJ XML is incomplete. Only works held in First1KGreek or Perseus editions are covered. odyCy's lemmas are a frozen 2026-07 run on First1K and a token-by-token run on the Perseus works; Dilemma is context-free on the surface; convention differences count against a system in the strict and blind columns and are partly undone in the article-neutral one. Relocation checked blind on the v0 loci (6,116) by two model readers on a salted sheet: fable-5-1 60/60 (100.0%, controls 10/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); opus-5-5 60/60 (100.0%, controls 10/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); agreement κ undefined (no variation: both readers said yes to every random locus). Relocation checked blind on the v0.3 loci (96,462) by two model readers on a salted sheet: opus-5-5 57/60 (95.0%, controls 9/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); sonnet-5-5 56/60 (93.33%, controls 10/10 anchors, 10/10 plants); agreement κ 0.8485. No human reader has judged a sheet yet; the sheets and keys are in the repository.
