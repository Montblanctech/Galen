Galen First Drafts

What the drafts say

678 items from the 13 works: people, lost authors and books, Galen's own life and writings, recipes, cases, procedures, prices and measures, and the places where the draft or the Greek itself may be wrong. Each item quotes the draft and links to the passage, with the Greek beside it. The page opens on the highlights; the order, from most to least interesting to a general reader, is a model reader's judgement, not a scholar's.

How this was made: model readers went through every passage of the machine drafts and noted what a researcher would want. A script then checked that every quotation is word for word in the draft and every Greek word is in that passage's Greek. Nothing else was checked. The summaries are the readers' and have not been read by a scholar, and the drafts themselves are unverified. Much of this has been read by specialists in the Greek; what is new is that it can now be read in English. Treat each item as a pointer to the passage, not as a finding to cite. Every item about a lost author or book, or about Galen's own works, was then checked by model researchers (not a specialist) against Fichtner's catalogue of Galen's works (2023), the standard fragment collections and the web: none was shown to be unknown to scholarship, a few could not be placed, and each such item says what was found and where. One, Galen's lost On the Innate Heat, seems to be known only from the passage shown here. A reading of 40 items chosen at random against the Greek (2026-11-11) found one summary that misstated the passage; it and the other problems that reading found are corrected and marked.
